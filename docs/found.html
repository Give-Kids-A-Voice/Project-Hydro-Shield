I am autistic and non-verbal.I may not answer, look at you, or come when called. Please stay with me and call my family.

How to help me

    If you can't reach anyone, call 911 and say: "I found a non-verbal autistic child." This card was made with Project Hydro-Shield from Give Kids A Voice.

    Make an "If found" card

    Fill this in, then write the link to a $1 NFC sticker on your child's shoe tag or bracelet. Anyone who taps it with a phone sees the card. Nothing is stored on any server: everything lives inside the link itself, so update the sticker if your numbers change.
