Where this works. Lakes, ponds and canals: all of Florida; Los Angeles, Orange, Ventura, Riverside, San Bernardino and San Diego counties in Southern California; Maricopa County, Arizona (Phoenix area); Clark County, Nevada (Las Vegas area); and Harris (Houston), Tarrant (Fort Worth/Arlington), Dallas and Collin (Plano/Frisco) counties in Texas. Florida pool records: Alachua, Brevard, Charlotte, Citrus, Collier, Duval, Hernando, Hillsborough, Indian River, Lake, Lee, Leon, Manatee, Marion, Martin, Orange, Osceola, Pasco, Pinellas, Polk, Sarasota, Seminole, St. Johns, St. Lucie and Volusia counties, with more coming. California pool records: San Diego County and the City of Palm Desert. Elsewhere in Southern California pool coverage is partial (OpenStreetMap): many backyard pools are not mapped yet. Arizona, Nevada and Texas pool records: Maricopa, Clark, Harris, Tarrant, Dallas and Collin counties. These come from assessor and appraisal-district records, which can lag a year behind new pools and do not show above-ground or unpermitted pools. Free, no sign-up, nothing you type is saved.

Palm Desert pool records: City of Palm Desert parcel data (Riverside County property tax records). Other Southern California pools: OpenStreetMap contributors (partial coverage). Arizona, Nevada and Texas water: OpenStreetMap contributors. Maricopa County pool records: Maricopa County Enterprise GIS parcel data. Clark County pool records: Clark County Assessor residential data and parcel map, as republished by the City of Las Vegas Office of GIS. Harris County pool records: Harris Central Appraisal District. Tarrant County pool records: Tarrant Appraisal District. Dallas County pool records: Dallas Central Appraisal District. Collin County pool records: Collin Central Appraisal District. Address search and satellite imagery by Esri; address search fallback by Nominatim. Not a substitute for supervision, door alarms or swim lessons.
