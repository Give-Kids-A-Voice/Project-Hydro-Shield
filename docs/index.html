For information only. This map is not guaranteed to be complete or accurate. Records can be out of date, some water is missing or slightly misplaced, and pools marked "aerial estimate" are automatic detections from aerial photos that can be wrong. Always walk and check the area around your home yourself. It is not a substitute for supervision, door alarms or swim lessons.
